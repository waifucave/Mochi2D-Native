The other development tools (TypeScript, Vite, esbuild, Vitest, Prettier
and their dependencies) are not included in any build. Playwright and
Chromium come from the environment and are not bundled either. PixiJS
(MIT) is a peer dependency of `dist/lib/pixi.js`, which imports it from the
app and does not include it; the repository installs it for its tests and
example only.

## musl

The float32 arctangent, sine and cosine in `src/core/math/libm32.ts` are
ported from musl's `src/math` (`atan2f.c`, `atanf.c`, `sinf.c`, `cosf.c`,
`__sindf.c`, `__cosdf.c`, `__rem_pio2f.c`, `__rem_pio2_large.c`). musl took
them from FreeBSD's msun; their float versions are by Ian Lance Taylor,
Cygnus Support, debugged and optimized by Bruce D. Evans.
